Assessment privacy

Updated October 16, 2026. This notice covers the current PQS screen and PBRS diagnostic preview.

Your answers

The current assessment processes answers in your browser. It does not submit them to RPE, a database, a payment processor or an AI service. It does not save answers to localStorage or sessionStorage. Refreshing or leaving the page loses the current assessment. Your browser, extensions, device and any files you choose to save remain under your control.

Downloads and printing

If you download the assessment record or print a report, it includes the answers, business name, goal and optional references you entered. Store and share it carefully. Use general record titles rather than passwords, bank details or personal customer information.

Website delivery

GitHub Pages and Cloudflare deliver the site and may process normal request information such as IP address, browser information and request times under their own policies. The assessment itself adds no analytics, external AI calls or third-party fonts. External links take you to sites with separate privacy practices.

Contact and earlier versions

If you email RPE, your message is processed through its email services. Do not send sensitive documents without an agreed secure route. Earlier versions used browser-local storage; clearing the current assessment does not erase those older records. You can remove older site data through your browser settings. If you previously purchased a PBS service or need privacy assistance, contact contact@risingphoenixhq.com.

This preview has no server-side account or assessment record for RPE to retrieve. We will update this notice before introducing saved accounts, paid delivery or additional processing.
