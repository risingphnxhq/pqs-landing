PBS · method 2.0.0

Simple signals. Visible reasoning.

Green, Yellow and Red help you orient. The value comes from understanding the supporting answers, what remains uncertain and what action is worth taking.

What we examine

Direction and decisions; customers and offer; cash and economics; delivery and quality; people and accountability; measurement and learning; controls and obligations; resilience and next-stage fit. Questions apply to a solo operator or team, and to evidence from a pilot or current operations. Sector-specific obligations still need appropriate specialist review.

How the index works

  1. Each question has four observable practice descriptions, scored 0–3. Unknown answers receive no invented value. Each area’s index is its answered points divided by its possible answered points. The overall descriptive index equally weights the eight areas and is shown only when each has at least one answer.
  2. An area is Red below 45; Yellow from 45 to below 75, or if incomplete; Green from 75 only when all its questions are answered. Grey means no information. These are original PBS design thresholds, not validated industry benchmarks.
  3. Overall Red means an area is Red or you report an urgent current risk. Green requires every area Green, every practice answer present and all four current risks explicitly answered No. Otherwise the position is Yellow; no practice answers and no urgent risk means insufficient information. A high average cannot hide a Red area.
  4. Records you name are references, not verified evidence. We separately display answered-question coverage, clarified risks, references and zero independent verifications. No revenue band, company size, entity form or text length adds points.
  5. Suggested actions come from the specific weaker answers you selected. They are working hypotheses and planning prompts. Choose an owner, baseline, target and review date; confirm whether a change actually helped. Completing a task does not automatically improve your score.

What the colors mean

Green supports investigating the next commitment; it does not mean “safe to scale” or a funding approval. Yellow calls for a focused improvement or missing information. Red calls for prompt attention to the identified issue. The screen does not predict failure, certify compliance or prove the cause of a business problem.

Research informing the design

PBS uses original questions and rules. It is not certified by, licensed as, or affiliated with these organizations. We studied their published descriptions, rather than copying questionnaires or scoring systems.

  • Baldrige Excellence Framework / NIST: structured organizational assessment and improvement; PBS separates declared practices from demonstrated outcomes.
  • EFQM Model: connecting direction, execution and results; PBS connects findings to an action and a later results check.
  • EOS: a practical view of operating components and accountability; PBS assigns a proposed owner and review point to each action.
  • GrowthWheel: broad screening to focus an advisory conversation; PBS identifies specific areas to investigate rather than prescribing the same package to everyone.
  • BDC digital maturity assessment: context and measured comparison; PBS does not invent a peer percentile without a relevant dataset.
  • B Lab / B Impact: distinction between self-assessment and verification; PBS labels references and verification status separately.
  • McKinsey 7-S: relationships across organizational elements; PBS asks customers to check interactions instead of assuming one isolated symptom explains everything.

Validation still required

This developmental rubric has software tests, not empirical predictive validation. Before offering an expert-equivalent paid service, RPE must complete independent practitioner review, sector/stage testing, blinded case comparisons, consistency checks and customer usefulness studies, and prove secure paid delivery. No claim of market leadership or guaranteed outcome is made.

Your information

The assessment makes no API or AI requests. Answers remain in memory and are lost on refresh. Downloads and printed reports contain your answers; keep them private. Hosting receives normal page requests. Use general titles for record references, not raw financial or customer information.
