PQS · Quick business check

Phoenix QuickScore

Eight questions to see where your business stands. Get a Green, Yellow or Red position, the reasons behind it, and practical next steps.

Choose answers you can support today. “I do not know” is useful information. Your business stage provides context; revenue size and the length of your answers do not earn points.

Clear position

Green: your answers describe strong practices.
Yellow: strengthen practices or clarify gaps.
Red: address the most pressing issues first.

Useful next steps

See the answers driving your result, records to consult and a proposed 30/60/90-day focus. An urgent risk overrides the average. Missing information cannot produce Green.

This quick check uses your answers to suggest where to focus. No one has independently checked those answers. Download or print your results before leaving or refreshing this page; otherwise they will be lost.
